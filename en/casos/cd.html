A loyalty club for
neighborhood businesses

Stamps on the phone, rewards within reach and WhatsApp messages that bring customers back.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Project

Personal · B2B2C

Platforms

App + mobile web

Tool

Figma

The problem

Neighborhood businesses don't know who comes back.

They don't know who's loyal

Or who stopped coming.

Paper punch cards get lost

And they leave no data.

They can't remind customers

Without paying for ads.

The question

How might a small business reward and win back customers with no ads, no apps to download and no change to how the counter works?

What I researched

In Peru, context shapes the product.

WhatsApp over email

It's where customers actually read.

Web over app

Works on any Android phone, nothing to download.

Stamps don't depend on payment

Cash, Yape or card: it still counts.

Data with consent

Peru's Law 29733: opt in, and opt out anytime.

Key decisions

Simple for the owner.
Frictionless for the customer.

3

services = 1 reward. A reward within reach brings people back.

0

apps to download. Just a QR code at the counter.

1

WhatsApp number: the business's own, one customers already know.

5

steps for owners to set up their club on their own.

What I left out: stored balance (regulated) and Wallet-only cards.

How it works

Two platforms, one club.

Business app

Sets up the club, accepts stamps and rewards, and sees who comes back.

Customer web

Scans the business QR, collects stamps across all their clubs and redeems rewards.

WhatsApp

Welcome, stamp added, reward ready and "we miss you".

Visual identity

Color is earned.

The brand is calm by default. Color shows up when customers come back: every stamp is a color.

A card that fills with color

Aa DM Sans for body text
